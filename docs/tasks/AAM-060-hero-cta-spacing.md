# AAM-060 — Give the landing hero CTA room to breathe

## Problem

The five-star-chef headline can wrap to three lines on narrow screens, leaving the primary CTA visually crowded against it.

## Acceptance criteria

- A clear, consistent gap separates the hero headline and “Let’s talk food” action.
- The CTA remains visible and usable on desktop and narrow screens.

## Out of scope

No copy, form, administrator, database, authentication, or deployment changes.

## Validation

Inspect the hero at desktop and narrow widths with the existing local preview.
